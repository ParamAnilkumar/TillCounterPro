TillCounter Pro

Privacy Policy

✅ No data leaves your device

Overview

TillCounter Pro ("the App") is a cash counting and till management tool for retail and hospitality teams. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

TillCounter Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or business data. All information you enter is stored exclusively on your device.

Data We Store (On Your Device Only)

The App stores the following data locally using SQLite and AsyncStorage on your device:

Data Type Purpose Sent Externally?
Till names & float amounts Till configuration Never
Count session totals & timestamps History & reports Never
Denomination quantities per session Denomination breakdown Never
Manager name (optional) Session attribution Never
App preferences (currency, dark mode) User preferences Never
Draft counts in progress Temporary state between screens Never

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address)
  • No device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, serial number)
  • No location data
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting sent to third parties
  • No advertising or marketing data
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts access

Internet & Permissions

TillCounter Pro requests no device permissions. The App does not make any network requests and does not require an internet connection to function. There is no backend server, no account system, and no cloud sync.

Data Export

The App provides optional CSV and PDF export features. These exports are generated entirely on your device and shared only via your device's native share sheet (e.g. email, messaging, file manager). TillCounter Pro does not receive a copy of any exported data.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can delete individual history entries within the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes all associated data from your device permanently.

Third-Party Services

TillCounter Pro does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services. The App is built using the Expo framework. No Expo services that transmit user data are used in this App.

Children's Privacy

TillCounter Pro is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the App's data practices change in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing for TillCounter Pro.
